Files in this folder

  1. start.cmd: starts the office (double-click). The office opens in its own window.
  2. install.cmd: first-time setup (double-click). Checks Node.js 22 or newer, offers the desktop shortcut and Start with Windows, then starts the office. Safe to run again.
  3. uninstall.cmd: stops the office and removes its shortcuts and the Start office link. Your files stay: delete the folder yourself to remove it completely.
